# 🛒 Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution designed to analyze retail sales, profitability, product performance, customer behavior, regional performance, and overall business growth.

The dashboard transforms retail sales data into actionable business insights through interactive KPIs, charts, slicers, customer analysis, product analysis, regional analysis, and detailed performance tables.

The goal of this project is to provide a centralized analytical view of retail business performance and help users monitor sales, profit, customers, products, regions, and operational trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard is designed to answer key retail business questions:

- What is the total sales generated?
- What is the total profit and profit margin?
- How many orders and quantities were sold?
- How is monthly sales performance changing over time?
- Which product categories generate the highest sales?
- Which products are the top performers by sales?
- Which products have the lowest profit?
- Which regions generate the highest profit?
- Which cities generate the highest sales?
- Which customers contribute the most sales?
- How does sales performance differ across customer segments?
- How does discount percentage affect profitability?
- Which sub-categories generate the highest profit?
- What are the key product-level performance metrics?
